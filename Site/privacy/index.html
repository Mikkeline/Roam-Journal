Roam Journal — Privacy Policy

Updated 26 November 2026

Roam Journal is a private travel journal. The developer does not operate a service that receives your journal content. This build saves your journal on your device; optional iCloud sync is disabled.

Your journal

Trips, notes, photos, flight details, packing lists, document copies, and recovery copies are encrypted on your device using a key in the device Keychain. Face ID controls access in the app. Trip names, dates, and counts can be visible on the trip list. Face ID is provided by Apple; the app does not receive your biometric template. Encryption does not prevent screenshots, a compromised device, or content you explicitly share.

Photos, documents, and ticket scanning

You select the photos and files you want to use. Imported copies stay inside your encrypted journal; the originals are unchanged. Ticket text recognition runs on your device. We do not upload tickets to an AI service. File-provider apps you select may download files under their own policies.

Location and maps

Only tapping Use my location requests location access. There is no background location tracking. Apple Maps receives searches and map requests, and a search region if you choose to use your location. Apple's privacy policy applies. Saved coordinates are encrypted in your journal; displaying a map needs a connection.

Airport and airline reference data

Airport and airline matching works offline. If you choose to update reference lists, the app requests public files from GitHub, whose normal connection metadata and privacy practices apply. No trip content is sent in those requests.

Backups and sharing

Encrypted backups go to the Files destination you choose. Keep the separate recovery code safe: the developer cannot recover a lost code. Anyone with the backup and its code can decrypt it. Text sharing creates an unencrypted copy after you choose to share. Receiving apps and services apply their own policies.

Retention, deletion, and recovery

Deleted trips and entries are retained in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days on the device. You can remove recovery copies earlier. Rolling encrypted storage snapshots may temporarily retain prior data; deletion is not a claim of forensic erasure. Exported backups and shared copies remain wherever you placed them until you delete them there. Losing the device or uninstalling without a usable backup can lose local-only data.

Optional iCloud builds

If a future supported build enables iCloud, the app will ask before uploading journal content to your private CloudKit database. Apple's account, storage, and recovery policies apply. Turning sync off does not remove existing cloud copies.

Tracking and diagnostics

This app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request advertising tracking permission. If you test a TestFlight build or choose to send diagnostics through Apple, Apple's diagnostics and TestFlight privacy practices apply. Do not include private journal contents when reporting a problem.

Contact and updates

Use the developer support channel published with the app's distribution listing for privacy questions. The public privacy-policy and support URLs must be published and configured before an App Store release. This policy must be reviewed if features or data handling change.
