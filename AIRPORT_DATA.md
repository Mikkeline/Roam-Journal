# Airport catalog

Offline IATA-coded airport subset of [mwgg/Airports](https://github.com/mwgg/Airports), downloaded 26 November 2026. The source is MIT-licensed; full license is included in RoamJournal/Resources/AirportData-LICENSE.txt. Timezone data originated from TimeZoneDB (https://timezonedb.com), and is updated upstream with TimeAPI (https://www.timeapi.io), as credited by the upstream project. Airport names and timezone data may become outdated; this is reference data, not live flight/terminal information.

KLIA aliases are app additions: KLIA T1 / KLIA1 maps to KUL Terminal 1; KLIA T2 / KLIA2 maps to KUL Terminal 2. Both share KUL / WMKK and Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Terminal suggestions should be verified against the ticket. Source: Malaysia Airports terminal pages https://www.malaysiaairports.com.my/en/klia1 and https://www.malaysiaairports.com.my/en/klia2 .
